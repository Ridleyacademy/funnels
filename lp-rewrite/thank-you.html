The Questions Everyone Asks

Stephen answers each one on video, watch them before we speak.

What is the Piano Masterclass?

The Piano Masterclass is Stephen's flagship self-study program, the one more than 40,000 adults have used to go from zero to playing the songs they love. In this short answer he walks you through exactly what's inside, how the method works, and why it gets complete beginners playing real music in days, not years.

How much does it cost?

Most people are shocked when they learn what it costs to learn piano for real the traditional way. Weekly lessons year after year, or a conservatory like Berklee or the Royal School of Music, can run $50,000 to $150,000 over 10 to 20 years.

Our programs are a fraction of that, starting at just $3,000. Stephen breaks down exactly how the pricing works in this answer, and on your call we'll find the right fit for your goals and budget.

Is this program for adults?

Yes. Everything here was built for adults. Most of our students are over 50, we teach players from 8 to 96, and we've found adult learners often progress faster: more time, more patience, and a lifetime of songs they've always wanted to play.

How long does it take?

The question everyone asks, and the one Stephen says we really excel at. He walks you through how quickly you can expect to be playing songs you actually love, rather than scales and exercises for years first.

The different programs we offer

From the self-study Piano Masterclass, the flagship more than 40,000 students have taken, all the way up to personal coaching with a world-class artist guiding you step by step. Stephen walks through each path here, and on your call we'll find the one that fits your goals and budget.

What if I don't have a piano?

Not a problem, it's actually not a bad thing. Every program includes Stephen personally guiding you to the perfect instrument for your budget, from free pianos on Facebook Marketplace to keyboards at every price point. We've thought of every single step.

My hands don't cooperate. Can I still play?

Yes. Stephen developed a revolutionary approach called Hand Yoga: a series of hand exercises you can do anywhere, any time, that build the coordination and dexterity to play like a pro with just a few minutes of practice a day.

Who is Stephen Ridley

More Than A Musician. An Artist On A Mission.

A professional pianist for over 10 years, Stephen has performed in over 70 countries at iconic events including the Monaco Grand Prix. With over a million followers and more than a billion views across social media, his central passion is simple: helping everyone access the joy and freedom of music, the same freedom that took him from a little boy in a village to stages around the world.
