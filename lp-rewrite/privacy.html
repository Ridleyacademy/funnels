Information you give us. When you fill out a form on this site we collect the details you enter, such as your name, email address, and phone number, along with your answers to any qualifying questions. When you purchase a program, payment is handled by secure third-party payment processors; we do not store your full card number.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, we and our service providers may collect standard technical data such as your device type, browser, pages visited, and general usage information, using cookies, local storage, and similar technologies.

By providing your phone number, you agree that we may contact you by phone, text message (SMS), or messaging apps about your booking and the services you have requested, and, where you have consented, with related offers. Message frequency varies and message and data rates may apply. You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP, or by writing to us at the address below.

We do not sell your personal information. This site uses local storage to remember details you have entered (for example, so your name and email are pre-filled when you book a call) and embeds third-party services, such as our scheduling provider, that may set their own cookies. You can control or clear cookies and site data through your browser settings; some features may not work as smoothly without them.

Advertising and measurement tools. This site also uses the Meta Pixel, a tool provided by Meta Platforms, Inc. (Facebook and Instagram), across its pages. It uses cookies and similar technologies to record how our pages are used and which steps are completed (for example, that a consultation was booked), so that we can measure how well our advertising works and show relevant ads to people similar to those who book with us. Meta may act as an independent controller of some of this data under its own terms.

You can limit this in several ways. You can adjust your ad preferences in your Facebook or Instagram account settings, use the industry opt-out pages at youradchoices.com or youronlinechoices.eu, turn on your browser's tracking protection, or use a content blocker. Blocking these tools does not affect your ability to use this site, book a call, or purchase.
