Your first step

Don't study piano. Sit down and play it.

You didn't buy this so you could learn four chord names. You bought it because somewhere in your head there is a song you would love to sit down and play. So that is where we're going.

Stephen is going to give your hands a small set of patterns they can actually use, then show you how those patterns begin turning into music you recognise.

No sheet music to decode first. No long theory lesson. No waiting months for something that sounds like a song.

Put your phone or laptop beside the piano and start.

Take Me To My First Lesson

4 Magic Chords

Four short lessons. Play along with each one.

Take them in order, and play as you watch rather than watching first and playing later. Each one is under ten minutes and works on a keyboard, an upright, or whatever you already own.

Lesson one

The shape your hands start from

You play in the first two minutes. This is the anchor everything else moves away from and returns to.

Lesson two

The one that creates the pull

On its own it is unremarkable. Next to the first one it is the reason a song sounds like it is going somewhere.

Lesson three

The one that carries the feeling

Lesson four

The one that takes you home

The resolution. With this in place the four become a loop, which is when a chord exercise turns into an actual song.

Then something interesting happens

Songs start looking different.

At first, a song can feel like hundreds of notes you somehow have to remember. Stephen wants you to begin seeing something else.

  • Patterns
  • Shapes
  • Movement
  • Relationships between sounds
  • Things that repeat from song to song

That's why a relatively small amount of musical understanding can suddenly unlock far more than one exercise or one piece.

You aren't trying to memorise four chords so you can say you know four chords. You're beginning to learn the language underneath the music you already love.

And at the live masterclass, Stephen takes that idea much further.
