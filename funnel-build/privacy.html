Effective Date: 13 August 2025

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

Your use of our Services and this Site may also be governed by those of our technology providers, including Kajabi, LLC. Please review Kajabi's Terms of Use and Privacy Policy at https://kajabi.com/policies, which also apply to your use of this Site.

1. Information We Collect

We collect two types of information:

  • Personal Information You Provide: Such as your name, email address, mailing address, phone number, payment information, demographic details, and preferences, when you register for our courses, complete forms, or make a purchase.
  • Automatic Information: Such as your IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers, referring URLs, and usage patterns collected via cookies and similar technologies.

2. Use of Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process your purchases, payment plans, and refunds in accordance with our Terms & Conditions
  • Deliver course content, mentorship programs, and related Services
  • Communicate with you regarding your account, purchases, and customer service inquiries
  • Improve our Site, Services, and customer experience
  • Send marketing communications if you have opted in (you may opt out at any time)
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our agreements

We do not sell or lease your personally identifiable information to third parties.

3. BNPL & Payment Processing Disclosure (Affirm, Klarna, and Other Providers)

If you choose to finance your purchase through Affirm, Klarna, or any other "Buy Now, Pay Later" (BNPL) provider we partner with:

  • Your personal and payment information will be shared with that provider for the purposes of processing your financing application, performing identity verification, and servicing your payment plan.
  • Each BNPL provider has its own privacy policy and terms, which you should review before completing your purchase.
  • We do not control the data practices of these providers.

4. Sharing of Information

We may share your personal information with:

  • Service Providers: Including hosting providers, payment processors, BNPL providers, and email marketing platforms.
  • Legal & Compliance Purposes: When required to comply with applicable laws, respond to lawful requests, or protect our legal rights.
  • Business Partners: When necessary to deliver joint promotions or services you have requested.

5. Children's Privacy

Our Services are not directed to children under the age of 16. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 16 without parental consent, we will delete it.

6. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies to:

  • Remember your login and preferences
  • Analyze Site traffic and usage patterns
  • Deliver relevant content and promotions

You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but some Site functions may not work properly. For more information on cookies used by Kajabi, see Kajabi's Cookie Policy.

7. Data Transfers & Storage

Your information may be stored and processed in the United States or other jurisdictions where our service providers are located. We take commercially reasonable measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access and disclosure.

8. Access, Updates, and Deletion Requests

You may request to review, update, or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing support@ridleyacademy.com with the subject line "Privacy Request." We will respond in accordance with applicable law.

9. California Privacy Rights

If you are a California resident, you may request information about how we have handled your personal information in the past year by emailing support@ridleyacademy.com with the subject "California Privacy."

10. Security

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a new "Effective Date."
