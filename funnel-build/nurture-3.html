Glenwood started at 68

One year in, he plays with both hands and reads music.

Real students, in their own words · 8:02

Glenwood Clark was 68 when he started with us. He wasn't chasing a stage or a recital. He just wanted to play. Within one year he had learned to play with both hands and read music.

Notice what that year wasn't: it wasn't scales and drills waiting for permission to touch real music. It was a year of playing, with the reading and the coordination built along the way.

We're not promising you Glenwood's exact year. Everyone moves at their own pace. But if you've been telling yourself it's too late, he's a useful counterexample.
