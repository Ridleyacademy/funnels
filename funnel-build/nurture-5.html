Why guided coaching is the difference between "someday" and actually playing

Here's the honest version. The hard part of learning piano as an adult isn't information. Lessons, videos, and apps are everywhere, and plenty of them are good. The hard part is follow-through: still practicing in week six, knowing whether the thing you just played was right, and having someone catch the small habits before they set.

That's what mentorship fixes. In our coached programs, hundreds of adults work through the Ridley Method with a coach who hears them play, gives real feedback, and keeps the path in front of them. Not more content. Accountability, and a person who notices.

For scale: two years of weekly private lessons runs $5,200 to $10,400, and the teacher still shows up with a method book from your childhood. We'd rather show you a different way to spend that ambition.
