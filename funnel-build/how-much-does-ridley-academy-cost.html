Pricing, honestly

How much does Ridley Academy cost? The full breakdown.

If you're searching for the price of Ridley Academy, you're probably asking three things:

  • How much does it actually cost?
  • What do you get for it?
  • Is it worth it compared to lessons or an app?

Fair questions. Here's the honest breakdown.

What is Ridley Academy?

Ridley Academy teaches adults to play real piano, starting with real music instead of years of scales and drills. The method was built by Stephen Ridley, a professional pianist who has taught 40,000+ students across 60+ countries, ages 5 to 96. Most of our students are over 50, and most arrive after a false start or two: childhood lessons that didn't stick, apps that trained them to follow falling notes without ever really playing.

So what does it cost?

There are two ways to learn with us, and they're priced very differently.

$27

The 4 Magic Chords Mini Course

The starting point. One payment, and it includes the 100+ Song Workbook free. It comes with a 30-day money-back guarantee, and you keep the workbook even if you refund. If you want to test whether this way of learning works for you, this is the low-risk way in.

Priced on your Breakthrough Session

Coached programs

Our coached programs pair you with structured mentorship through the full method. Pricing depends on the level of support you choose, which is why we don't publish a single number here. The right way to find out is the Breakthrough Session: a free 30-minute conversation about where you are, what you want to play, and which path fits. It's a conversation, not a checkout. You never have to play a note, and there's no obligation on the call.

The comparison that actually matters

Here's the math most people never do. Two years of weekly private lessons at $50 to $100 per lesson runs $5,200 to $10,400. That's the traditional route, and it usually front-loads years of technique before you play music you love. Apps look cheaper until you count the years of subscription fees against a simple question: can you sit down at a real piano, without the screen, and play?

When you weigh our coached programs on your Breakthrough Session, weigh them against that.

What's included in the coached programs

  • A structured path through the full Ridley Method: your first song, then real repertoire, then musical fluency, then your own sound
  • Personal feedback and mentorship (the follow-through layer that apps and YouTube can't give you)
  • A community of adult learners in the same season of life
  • Lifetime access to the course material

Why we don't do "quick fix" pricing

Learning an instrument is a 12-month arc, not a 21-day challenge. We structure programs around the full journey because that's what actually gets an adult from "someday" to playing every day. If a program promises piano in three weeks, ask them to show you a student playing at a party.

Frequently asked questions

How much do piano lessons cost compared to Ridley Academy?

Weekly private lessons typically run $50 to $100 per lesson, which is $5,200 to $10,400 over two years. Our coached programs are discussed on your Breakthrough Session so we can match the level of support to you. More on lesson costs in our piano lesson cost guide.

Is there a cheap way to start?
