Why adults learn faster than kids

The head start you didn't know you had.

Meet Stephen · 1:41

Most adults assume they missed their window. The thinking goes: kids soak music up, adults just grind. In our experience it runs the other way. Adult learners often progress faster: more time, more patience, and a lifetime of songs they've loved.

A kid at a piano is playing someone else's assignment. You sit down with thirty years of music already in your head. You know exactly how the songs you love are supposed to sound, and that turns out to be a real advantage, not a handicap.

That's why most of our students are over 50. Not because they're catching up. Because this is actually a good age to start.
