The sheet-music myth

You don't need years of theory before you're allowed to play real music.

Stephen's story · 4:24

Traditional lessons hand you a deal: years of scales, drills, and childhood method books first, real music later. Maybe. A lot of adults took that deal once, stalled out, and decided the problem was them.

It's not you. It's the method. You don't need years of scales before real music; you need a path that starts with the music. The Ridley Method puts a real song under your hands first, then lets the theory land through the music you're already playing. Theory makes sense after you can play. Before that, it's just homework.

The same goes for the falling-note apps. They're games. They can keep you tapping for months without ever making you a player. A song you can actually sit down and play does more for you than either.
