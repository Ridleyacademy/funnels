One template, re-angled per keyword. Sales page wearing SEO clothing: indexed, keyword-targeted, full persuasion arc, Apply CTA. (From teardown 05, section B2.)

## The five landers to build

| Slug | Target keyword | H1 angle |
|---|---|---|
| /online-piano-lessons-adults | online piano lessons for adults | "Piano Lessons Built for Adults Who Are Done Starting Over" |

## Section order (identical on all five)

1. Pain-led H1 + subhead (keyword in both)
2. "Why traditional [keyword category] fails adults" (3 short blocks: built for kids/exams, theory-first kills momentum, no follow-through layer)
3. The Ridley difference (belief flip: it's not you, it's the method)
4. The method (4 phases: First Song, Real Repertoire, Musical Fluency, Your Sound)
5. Proof: stats row (40,000+ / 60+ countries / ages 5 to 96 / 1,300+ reviews) + 2 named testimonials (rotate: David Clarke, Glenwood Clark, Carlos Rodriguez, Alice Saddler)
6. Comparison table: apps vs. private lessons vs. Ridley (reuse offramp.html table)
7. Who this is for (self-qualification, keyword-matched)
8. FAQ, keyword-rich (5 questions, schema markup)
9. About Ridley Academy authority block (Stephen bio, press strip)
10. CTA: quiz for cold keywords, Apply/Breakthrough Session for cost-intent keywords

## Rules

- Real numbers from FACTS.md only. No em or en dashes. No hype.
- Cost-intent pages anchor with "two years of weekly lessons runs $5,200 to $10,400" and never print coaching prices.
- Refresh-in-place on the same slug each year (their dated-slug equity play).
- Indexed (these are the exception to the funnel noindex rule).
